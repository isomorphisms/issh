# Thanks

This mirror exists because SSH was specified in public documents and kept
maintainable across decades.

Thanks to the authors and editors named in every mirrored RFC; to Tatu Ylonen
and Chris Lonvick for the core SSH protocol documents; to the SECSH, CURDLE,
and SSHM working-group participants; to the RFC Editor for preserving the
canonical series; to IANA for maintaining the live SSH parameter registries;
and to the IETF Trust for making the redistribution terms explicit.

Later specifications also owe substantial practical input to implementers and
operators across OpenSSH, libssh2, and other SSH implementations. Their names
and organizations remain credited in the individual RFC acknowledgements and
author sections; those original credits are preserved verbatim in `text/`.
